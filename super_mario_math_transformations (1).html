↔️ 1. TRANSLATION (Slide/Shift)

Moving a shape without flipping or spinning it. Jump on the Red Mushroom to slide horizontally ($\Delta x$), or step on the Blue Mushroom to lift straight up ($\Delta y$)!

🔄 2. ROTATION (Turn around a point)

Spinning an object around its central point. Bump the Super Stars from below to watch them spin $360^\circ \times 3 = 1080^\circ$ total before you collect them!

🪞 3. REFLECTION (Mirror Image)

Flipping an object across a reflection axis. Watch Mario's horizontal shadow mirror across the line ($x = 400$) and his vertical flipped image in the bottom water lake!
